Why choose Alu instead of another flashcard software?

Alu's free spaced repetition immediately gives it an edge over any other non-spaced repetition software.
On top of this, Alu has built-in note-taking capabilities, allowing you to take notes and study all in the same place.
Alu has a more social experience than many other flashcard apps, allowing you to find a group of friends that are dedicated to learning similar subjects as you.

Spaced Repetition

What is spaced repetition?
In spaced repetition, the concepts that are most difficult to you are shown to you the most. All you need to do is rate how hard it was to answer a flashcard, and our algorithms will automatically optimize when you should see the flashcard next.

In our opinion, any flashcard program without spaced repetition is no better than pencil and paper

What spaced repetition algorithm do you use?
With Alu, you can choose your spaced repetition algorithm.
We have "Anki" spaced repetition, with built-in presets for more optimal learning, and we plan to implement more powerful algorithms such as "SM-18" soon.
