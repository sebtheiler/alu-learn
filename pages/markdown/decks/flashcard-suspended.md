## Suspended Flashcards
Suspended flashcards will not be shown to you when you study.  This feature makes it easy to temporarily disable flashcards that need to be improved or edited.

### Marking as suspended
You can mark a flashcard as suspended when browsing the flashcard's parent deck through the "Suspend" button.  Suspended flashcards will be displayed as a light yellow color, making them easily distinguishable from regular flashcards, and their red [leech](/help/leeches/) counterparts.

Alternatively, you can mark a flashcard as suspended while studying it by clicking the three bars in the lower-right-hand corner of the screen.
