## Leech Flashcards
Leech flashcards are flashcards that Alu has automatically detected you have difficulty learning.

It is recommended that you pay special attention to these cards, such as reviewing the material or rephrasing the question. For help rephrasing the flashcard, you may want to see the [creating good flashcards](/help/creating-good-flashcards/) tutorial.

### Detection
By default, flashcards are marked as leeches after you have answered "Again" on eight separate days.  When a flashcard is marked as a leech, the `leech` tag is added to its list of tags (this does not destroy previously existing tags). When a flashcard has the leech tag, it is displayed with a light red color in the flashcards browse section, differentiating it from [suspended flashcards](/help/suspended), which are displayed as light yellow.

### Marking as a leech
It possible to manually mark a flashcard as a leech by adding the `leech` tag to it, or by clicking "Mark as leech" in the three-bars-expand-menu (bottom right) when studying.
