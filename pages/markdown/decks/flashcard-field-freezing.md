## Freezing Fields in Flashcard Creation
When creating flashcards you can click the snowflake icon to "freeze" a field.  Freezing a field makes its value stay constant, even after you create the flashcard. When a field is frozen, the snowflake icon next to it will appear a shade of blue - otherwise, the snowflake will appear gray.

This can be incredibly helpful when creating multiple similar flashcards, or creating flashcards with identical tags.
